1. Cuunfaa Hojii (Executive Summary)

Sanadni kun ijaarsa Al-Furqaan Ultimate Global Markaz Management System tiif qajeelfama hojii, ulaagaalee hojii (functional requirements), ijaarsa tekinikaa, database design, nageenya odeeffannoo, afaanota, platform deployment fi karoora raawwii kaa’a.

Kaayyoon isaa hojii barattootaa, Hifz, attendance, qormaata, Mu’allimoota, maatii, kaffaltii, Kafalat, donation, admission, kitaabota, hojjettoota, gabaasa fi bulchiinsa waliigalaa application tokko keessatti walitti fiduudha.

Sanadni kun blueprint dha; application guutuun hojiirra jira jechuu miti. Faayiloonni jalqabaa React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Firebase, Firestore, Google Gemini SDK fi meeshaalee PDF/QR/chart fayyadamuuf qophaa’an agarsiisu. Garuu source code guutuun (fakkeenyaaf src/main.tsx fi components) fi qajeelfamni Firestore rules guutuun hin mul’anne; kanaaf implementation dhugaa dura codebase guutuu audit gochuun dirqama.

6. Functional Modules – Ulaagaalee Hojii

6.1 Student Information & Registration

· Student ID/code ofumaan uumuu; duplicate detection (maqaa, bilbila maatii, date of birth).

· Full name, photo, age/date of birth, gender, guardian, phone, address, enrollment date, track, halaqah, status.

· Student profile keessatti attendance, Hifz logs, exams, tuition, sponsorship fi disciplinary/support notes (access-controlled) walitti agarsiisuu.

· Student transfer, suspension, withdrawal, graduation fi archive workflows.

6.2 Hifz Progress – Sabaq, Sabqi & Manzil

· Guyyaa, student, Mu’allim, sabaq range/page, current surah, sabqi juz, manzil juz, grade fi remarks galmeessuu.

· Daily entry, weekly/monthly summary, revision consistency fi progress trend.

· Barataa sadarkaa gadi aanaa, attendance hir’ate ykn manzil irraa hafaa jiru alert godhuu.

· Juz 1–30 fi Surah list sirrii; terminology fi grading criteria Markazaan raggaasifamu.

6.3 Attendance

· Attendance barataa guyyaa/shift/halaqah irratti Present, Absent, Late, Excused.

· Attendance Mu’allimoota fi staff addatti hordofuu.

· Monthly percentage, absence streak, reason fi guardian follow-up.

· QR attendance optional; QR code akka token nageenya qabu ta’u, ID qofa scan gochuun hin gahuu.

6.4 Examination & Assessment

· Exam type: Hifz oral, Tajweed, revision, written/basic Islamic studies.

· Exam date, juz/surah tested, examiner, score, grade, comments fi moderation.

· Grading scale admin settings keessatti qindeessuu; weighted score yoo barbaachise ifatti ibsuu.

· Result approval, report card, ranking (policy eeyyame qofa) fi historical comparison.

6.5 Halaqah & Academic Scheduling

· Halaqah groups, Mu’allim assignment, capacity, timetable, classroom/location.

· Student movement between groups with effective date and history.

· Group-level progress, attendance and teacher workload reports.

6.6 Staff & Mu’allim Management

· Staff profile, role, contact, qualification, employment status, contract and assigned responsibilities.

· Attendance, leave, timetable, performance review and optional payroll integration.

· Sensitive HR data only HR/authorized management access.

6.7 Tuition, Finance & Receipts

· Fee plan, student invoice, monthly payment, partial payment, balance and arrears.

· Receipt number unique and sequential; void/reversal workflow with reason and approver.

· Cash/bank/mobile payment channels; daily cash reconciliation and ledger.

· Financial reports by month, category, class, payer and status.

6.8 Kafalat & Sponsorship

· Orphan/eligible beneficiary profile and sponsorship eligibility, subject to privacy controls.

· Sponsor record, pledge, assigned student, monthly commitment, payment history and outstanding period.

· Matching, renewal, sponsor communication and anonymized public reporting.

· Prevent double sponsorship allocation and track unallocated donations.

6.9 Donation & Fundraising

· Donor profile, pledge, actual received donation, campaign, date, payment channel and receipt.

· Separate pledge from received cash; pledge is not revenue until received.

· Campaign target, amount raised, remaining balance, donor acknowledgement and export.

· Anonymous donor option and donor data visibility controls.

6.10 Admission & Public Portal

· Public application form with validation and consent notice.

· Application reference number, status: submitted, under review, accepted, waitlisted, rejected.

· Registrar review, document checklist, contact and conversion to Student record.

· Public form must never expose student collection or internal admin data.

6.11 Parent / Guardian Portal

· Guardian securely links to one or more children through verified relationship.

· View only approved attendance, progress, exam result, notices and permitted fee statements.

· Guardian access revocation, phone change verification and consent management.

6.12 Library & Learning Resources

· Book/catalogue, author, category, language, copy count, accession code.

· Borrowing, return, due date, overdue, loss/damage and inventory.

· Access by librarian; student borrowing history limited by policy.

6.13 Certificate & Graduation

· Graduation cohort, completion criteria, review and approval.

· Certificate number unique; Arabic/Oromo/English templates.

· QR verification page must show only minimum certificate verification details.

· Reprint/correction log and revoked certificate handling.

6.14 Communication & Notifications

· In-app notices, SMS/email/WhatsApp integration only after provider and consent are configured.

· Templates in three languages; delivery status and failed message retry.

· Do not send sensitive student or finance data in unsecured notifications.

6.15 Reports & Analytics

· Daily/weekly/monthly/annual academic and administrative reports.

· Student counts by gender, track, status, halaqah and enrollment cohort.

· Hifz progress, attendance, exam trends, teacher workload, fees, sponsorship and donations.

· Filters, date ranges, CSV/XLSX/PDF export, print layouts and role-specific dashboards.

6.16 AI Assistant

· AI can draft narrative reports, summarize approved aggregate data and suggest follow-up lists.

· AI must not independently change student grades, financial records, admission decisions or user permissions.

· Use server-side Gemini API key in Cloud Functions/secure backend; never commit secrets to source control.

· Send minimum necessary data; mask names/phone numbers for aggregate analysis; log AI actions and require human review.

10. Security, Privacy & Governance

· Firestore Rules: default deny; authenticated user qofa jechuun data hunda arguu hin danda’u.

· Role-based + record-level authorization: Mu’allim student halaqah isaa qofa; guardian child isaa qofa.

· Admin privilege least-privilege; privileged account MFA fi account recovery qindaa’uu.

· Sensitive operation (grade edit, payment reversal, role change, certificate revoke) audit log fi approval barbaada.

· Firebase Storage rules: student photos/documents private; signed/authorized access qofa.

· API keys: Firebase web config public client configuration ta’uu danda’a, garuu Gemini secret/API key server-side qofa tursiisi; source code keessatti hin commit’in.

· Backups: scheduled export, restore drill, retention schedule fi disaster recovery owner.

· Data minimization: odeeffannoo daa’immanii, guardian phone fi financial records barbaachisummaa irratti hundaa’uun qofa kuusi.

· Privacy notice, consent, retention/deletion policy fi incident response procedure qopheessi.

· Security rules emulator/test suite irratti test godhi; production deploy dura independent review.

Faayiliin firestore.rules maqaan isaa project upload keessatti jira; qabiyyeen isaa sanadni kun keessatti mirkanaa’ee hin ilaalamne. Kanaaf rules amma jiran nageenya qabu jedhamee hin raggaasifamne.

13. Non-Functional Requirements

· Performance: common dashboard/list views paginated; indexes planned; avoid unbounded reads.

· Reliability: error states, retry strategy, transaction-safe payment and receipt creation.

· Offline: optional PWA caching for app shell; offline data entry queue only where conflict handling and encryption are designed.

· Scalability: use institutionId partitioning if future branches/centres are added.

· Maintainability: typed models, reusable components, validation schemas, automated tests, code review.

· Observability: structured logs, crash/error monitoring, uptime and usage metrics.

· Compatibility: current evergreen browsers; Android/iOS browser support; test low-bandwidth connections.

· Localization: RTL/LTR test coverage, date/number/currency formatting and translation completeness.
